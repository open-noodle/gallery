Nav dock prototype

The tab shell gets a dock that folds as you scroll. Pages opened from it, and search, keep working as they do today.

  • Photos: the grouping row sits above the tabs. Scroll down to fold both into one row. Scroll up or tap the circle to unfold.
  • Spaces and Library have no grouping row, so their dock folds to the circle and search.
  • Spaces, albums and people open over the tabs with today's pill: grouping plus a search button, no tab bar.
  • Search is the shipped header: field, People / When / Places / Tags / Filters chips, and the total with sort. On Photos the dock becomes the tab circle and the grouping row, pinned to the bottom. Tapping the field hides the dock, as the keyboard does in the app.
