Nav dock prototype

Every screen that shows photos uses the same dock: a grouping row above, and tabs plus search below. Only the photos and the search scope change from screen to screen.

  • Scroll down to fold the dock into one row. Scroll up or tap the circle to unfold it.
  • Spaces, albums, people open inside their tab, which stays highlighted. They get the same grouping row, and search looks only inside that space, album or person.
  • Search stretches into a field at the bottom. Results get the grouping row directly above the field, never in the middle of the screen. Scrolling the results tucks the row away.
  • The Spaces list and the Library page show no photos, so their dock has no grouping row.
