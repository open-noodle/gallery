Search C, deeper: filters that react to each other

Both phones start on today's Photos screen. Tap the round 🔍 button, bottom right. The top bar and bottom nav step aside; ← brings them back.

The fake library has about 3,500 photos from 120 trips and events, so the filters really narrow each other. Pick Places → Kyoto and watch People, When and Camera shrink to what exists in Kyoto, each with a count. Options with nothing left disappear. In the top field, @, # and city names work too.

The two phones differ only in how you reach the filters beyond People, When, Places and Tags.

C1 · Every filter in the chip row

One scrolling row holds all ten filters. A filter with nothing left to offer greys out. Everything is one tap away, but the row is long: Camera, Albums and Text sit off-screen to the right.

C2 · Main four, plus a Filters sheet

The row shows People, When, Places and Tags, then a Filters chip with a badge. That chip opens one tall sheet with everything else (favorites, photo or video, rating, camera, albums, text) and live counts. An active extra filter also shows as its own chip in the row, so you can see it and remove it.
