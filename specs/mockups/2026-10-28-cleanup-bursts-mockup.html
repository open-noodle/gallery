Bursts & series: three ways to see the frames bigger

All three keep today's logic: the sharpest frame is suggested, you pick the frames to keep, the rest go to trash, or you stack the series instead. They differ in how big the frames get and how many series you see at once.

A

Big frame + filmstrip, in the list

Each series shows its current frame large, with the whole series as a filmstrip underneath. Hovering or using ← → swaps the big frame, so you compare at full size. The list still scrolls through every series.

Side-by-side compare

The suggested best frame sits on the left and the frame you're looking at on the right, both large, with a zoom that stays in sync so you can check eyes or text at 100%. Good for picking between near-identical frames.

And in every option: open any frame to see it with its details

Each frame gets an expand button (and Space on the focused frame). It opens the same photo window as the Blurry and Screenshots queues: the photo large, its details, and a link to open it in a new tab. Inside a series, ← → step through that series' frames and K / Delete keep or trash the frame shown.
