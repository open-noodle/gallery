Trash: 4.2 GB
Build this hub. You reach it from Utilities → Cleanup; there is no sidebar item. Each queue row and the calendar load independently, so they fill in separately. Rows show a count and the space they could free. The Duplicates row opens the existing /utilities/duplicates page. Blurry shows the analysing state until quality analysis has covered the library; Bursts shows "up to N". Why calendar first: the whole library becomes one visible, finite map, and the dark squares show where the backlog is.
